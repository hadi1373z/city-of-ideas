# Six outdoor learning places

Six original open pavilions occupy the six outer hexagon centres at radius
478.046 m. They add working experiments rather than empty named houses. The
same source positions supply `CITY_PLACES`, `PLACE_PATHS` and
`CITY_PLACE_FOOTPRINTS`. The urban-road owner draws the 3.4 m spurs from each
entry at local Z+18 to its block vertex at Z+92, and excludes street trees from
the actual structural radius17 and doorstep rectangle. No trees are generated
by these modules.

The Graph Garden has an exhaustive four-node Euler experiment. The Probability
Pavilion changes a 2–60-person crowd and its exact birthday probability under
explicit independent, uniform365-day assumptions. The Listening Court is
silent: two40-ms magnified traces accompany a one-second sum and absolute beat
envelope. Mapmakers Square compares ordinary and doubly wrapped grid adjacency;
wrap steps terminate at paired boundary markers instead of drawing false long
physical route segments. Light Court constructs exact incoming and reflected
unit directions. Symmetry Garden gives the arrow design C_n or D_n symmetry
using genuinely one-handed bent petals and same-colour reflected copies.

All geometry, diagrams, buildings and English teaching text are original.
Source links identify Euler’s bridge argument, Harvard’s probability exercise,
OpenStax’s beats and reflection chapters, NetworkX’s periodic-grid definition
and MIT’s symmetry-group notes. No source image, external mesh or quotation is
reused. Roof silhouettes are distinct: paired garden arches, a lantern shell,
a raised saddle, a square pergola, a triangular sail and a radial crown.

`buildCityPlaces` owns its original geometry, one shared solid material, one
line material and six nameplate canvases. Static architecture and experiment
meshes are batched. Changing a control replaces and disposes only that site’s
dynamic geometry. It adds no lights, render target, animation, storage access or
network fetch. Every raised experiment has a matching obstacle; broad entry
and side routes remain clear. All structural geometry and colliders fit inside
the source radius17; only the level doorstep reaches Z+18.

`installCityPlaces` adds one shared reader and a dismissible touch-safe hint.
Study is available only in city mode, within20 m and at ground feet Y.13;
hidden documents, other readers and immersive sky observation block opening.
Controls are ordinary keyboard-accessible buttons and labelled range inputs.
All changes are manual, including under reduced-motion preferences. The reader
closes after leaving its physical site. `visit(id)` uses the real clear entry,
and `interact()` supports the host’s existing keyboard/controller action chain.
The optional **Carry this question** button calls the already installed
`M.visitorMemory.store.question` only on that explicit action. No question is
saved by arrival, opening, changing an experiment or automatic updates.

The installer restores borrowed method hooks and removes only its own targets,
UI and scene objects on disposal. The host installs the module/CSS and connects
its existing action chain and public destination map. Run
`node tests/city-places.mjs` for exhaustive small-model proofs, actual 3D
footprint/headroom/door tests, geometry ownership and UI/persistence boundaries.
