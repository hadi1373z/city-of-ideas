# Work with the object, then read the argument

The six seminar/study houses already contained substantial readings and working
models, but their full-screen readers obscured the objects being changed. This
update gives each interior a compact workbench and a separate full-reading view.
It develops existing houses rather than adding another destination.

- Noether: the orbit now carries velocity and restoring-force arrows from the
  exact solution. The common drawing scale is 0.4. Equal and unequal stiffness
  still load distinct autonomous systems, rather than claiming a physical spring
  was switched during motion.
- Mirzakhani: metric/path controls can be used beside the visible disk. The
  geodesic and the chord are still measured with the same hyperbolic metric.
- Cage: a cursor beside the physical sixteen-slot score and `aria-current` in
  the score follow `AudioContext.currentTime`, including rests. Sound remains
  opt-in. Stop, leaving the house, changing the seed and disposal clear it.
- Proof: either colour can be chosen as the left side of Hall's condition.
  Certificates are checked by the inequality, not by a preset's identity. The
  opposite-corners board has eight light squares with six neighbours. The
  balanced board still exposes the two-dark-to-one-light bottleneck. Maximum
  matching temporarily hides selection colours and restores the selected subset
  on return. A failed certificate never claims a board is tileable.
- Uncertainty and Computation: physical bars and machine states can be inspected
  while changing evidence assumptions and execution steps.

`room-workbench.js` shares four-language navigation labels and an explicit look
action. It changes orientation, never the visitor's location. It updates the
camera immediately because the normal camera loop is paused by a modal. The
compact dialog has a transparent backdrop, a right dock on desktop and a bottom
dock on narrow screens. Walking remains paused until the controls are closed.
The close control stays visible while scrolling.

Full readings, primary-source links and explicit notebook saves remain. Unsaved
reflections survive switching modes and languages; the advanced-house reflection
also retains its text selection during locale refresh. No speech, voices, private
storage keys, island coordinates or hourly-review changes are modified.

Verification covers both sides of Hall's condition, matching/selection round
trips, exact vector directions, real-camera orientation without movement,
audio-clock cursor changes, draft preservation, focus, locale direction and
resource disposal. Native browser inspection exercised all six houses, the
corner-board certificate, both evidence assumptions, a repeated machine state,
the curved geodesic and optional score playback. Narrow-screen inspection checked
the bottom dock and the visible room; temporary viewport settings were restored.

Remaining limits: the older three study houses still have simple rectangular
interiors. Their advanced reading and experiments remain English, with the
existing language disclosure. These are bounded experiments, not proof checking
for arbitrary graphs, an unrestricted program runner or complete courses.
