# Paper birds, a clear toolbar and the gallery-facing ascent

The two park companions are flying paper birds. Every visible bird surface belongs to one folded square: a doubled triangle, a body crease and two hinged wings. Extra cone bodies, legs, noses, eyes and floating nameplates were removed. The model contains eight paper panels, whose combined area equals the original square. Flight uses gentle wing motion, banking and hovering; birds pause nearby and respect reduced motion. Clicking a bird, pressing E nearby or using its proximity button opens its lesson.

The new seven-step bird lesson has original vector diagrams, front-layer folds, a turnover and an open-wing view. English, Persian and German instructions teach the same simplified paper form. The dog and cat face lessons and their existing completion keys remain in the lesson panel. The final instruction moves the physical bird by hand; animated flight is part of the city scene. A linked [traditional bird guide](https://origami.guide/?p=5433) offers another model; its photographs and diagrams were not copied.

The five utility buttons for content/tools, city map, philosophers, Socrates and origami friends no longer appear in the lower toolbar. Contextual gallery and ascent controls remain. Welcome-screen entries and in-world interactions continue to work.

The mountain spiral now starts at (82, 0), west of its centre and directly opposite the gallery door at (58, 0). The connecting path and entrance sign follow that axis. All four turns, chapter IDs and elevation values are retained. The summit bridge and return lift rotate with the entrance; the lift returns beside the gallery at (68, 0). At the summit the visitor faces the baby.
