by .38 × 3.15 × .7 columns at (±2.22,1.575,8.35). Its plaza plinth ends at

within those exact footprints bridge the interval.

The installed facade's five-metre connector paving already reaches the column
bases at logical Y.13. The adapter verifies that coverage and adds **no soles
in the installed world**. Its standalone source fallback supplies two soles
only when that actual support is absent. It does not duplicate the paving or
enlarge column/collider footprints. Native inspection corrected the initial
source-only assumption before publication.

Four .08-metre knee members fit the original column/header joints. Each starts
inside a column at source Y2.55; one reaches the header transversely at
x=±1.62/Y3.02 and one reaches the front overhang at z9.32/Y3.02. Ends actually
intersect the existing supports, inner members clear the central three metres
and the new braces remain above source Y2.50. These are digital construction
details, without a load calculation or building-code certification.

## Ownership, transforms and private writing

`math-wing-entrance.js` checks the original floor-zero concept IDs, exact
source canopy/columns/plinth/sign/roof objects, and the actual public installed
source name. The facade branch separately verifies its public owner flag,
nameplate, porch/roof/header/rail instances and source-to-facade transform.
It derives local coordinates from those owner matrices. The proper common
lower-parent half-turn cancels in the relative mapping; nothing is baked into
source coordinates, private imports or saved poses.

In the installed world the layer has **14 draw meshes**, three materials, two
shared geometry resources and one own canvas texture: six owned resources.
There are no new image assets, lights, colliders, instance batches, voices or
recordings. Standalone source rendering has eight meshes with the same six
owned resources. Missing/replaced source or facade owners restore the three
original visibility values and release owned resources once; borrowed source
and facade resources are never disposed. An unchanged language redraws nothing.

The earlier stair adapter remains separate, preserving its 77 retired source
meshes and eight removed/two added colliders. This layer retires two additional
source text planes and one facade text plane: **80 across the two adapters**.
Installed source collider counts remain ground24/upper15, with original
approach arrays. Doors, connector, all eight floors/24 IDs, Atlas/books, source
furniture/seats, solid windows, lift metadata and all eight companions remain.

The runtime wraps only the public update chain and language event. It owns no
movement, arrival, floor selection, picks, reader dialog, private input,
storage/memory, experiment/discovery, progress or audio. Raw drafts/carets/first
answers, private keys, imports/notes and explicit saves retain their owners.
Cleanup restores the update hook only while it owns it. The user's headed
preview remains under their control; native QA uses an independent browser.
