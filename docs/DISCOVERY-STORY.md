# The book of unfinished answers

Start in Socrates' study: face the reading book on the desk and press E, use the controller interaction button, or select the book. This is the real desk book; the journal, library and neighbouring homes retain their original actions. The opening also offers the desk's usual reading.

The optional authored story asks a question about recognition and understanding. Carry an answer and reason to Dalí's dream residence, examine the existing reproduction of The Persistence of Memory, distinguish observation from interpretation, then return to the study and keep or revise the answer. Destination buttons use existing safe arrival points; visitors can instead walk freely. Reflection requires returning to the study. The narrative is original fictional writing, not a historical quotation, live AI conversation or a new production voice.

The current reading and completed pages use the separate `city-discovery-story-v1` local-storage key. They do not replace the existing Socratic conclusion, learning journal, account history or private home data. Closing preserves the current place. A completed page is retained before a new reading starts; earlier pages can be reopened from the opening. Notes remain verbatim when switching English, German and Persian. A storage failure reports the limitation and keeps the current page in memory for copying during that visit; no cross-device saving is claimed.

The root dialog uses the existing keyboard and controller navigation, activation and Back controls. The additional nearby story action appears only at the relevant destination during an active reading. No global story-launch button or replacement city layout is added.
