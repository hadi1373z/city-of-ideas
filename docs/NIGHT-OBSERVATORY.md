# The central observatory

The lower Mathematics rotunda has a curved transparent roof, from the retained
r61 eaves at local Y16.4 to the r21.5 join at Y20.55, and a central dome reaching
Y29. Only the named old opaque annular roof is hidden; teardown restores it.
The existing lower world frame performs the physical half-turn. Night belongs
to the lower city; the upper city stays in daylight.

From the paper gallery's highest landing at Y12.13, eighty real 0.125-m treads
continue one turn at radius14.4..17.6 to Y22.13. An exact curved landing and
inward bridge reach a supported central deck, radius7.2. The historic well stays
open. Navigation and visible geometry use the same heights and boundaries.

The observatory controller runs before the paper-gallery and world controllers.
It returns ownership to the highest paper landing or descending stair, then the
same route reaches the western exit and original through-gallery. The full
runtime regression walks this entire journey in both directions, without using
a visit action or teleport. The explicit `?arrival=observatory` destination is
a separate way to begin on the supported deck.

Study requires lower-city mode, the real telescope height and distance within
four metres. E/A or the nearby action opens the reader. Aiming at a visible star
changes both the view and telescope tube. The guide is dismissible and modal
study pauses walking. Observatory look controls can reach near the zenith.

`night-sky-catalogue.json` contains 2,848 real bright-star positions adapted from
HYGv4.1. `night-sky-model.js` uses the explicit chart date, Prague coordinates,
precession and sidereal time. Six original guide figures support questions about
projection, rotation, brightness, latitude and horizons. Seasonal presets change
the teaching chart; the city remains night. IAU constellation areas are
distinguished from illustrative lines. Attribution, ShareAlike license,
modification notice and scientific limits remain in the visitor reader and
NIGHT-SKY-SOURCES.md.

Sky points and guide lines both recenter for each render camera, including
photos and the pond, preventing walking parallax. They remain depth-tested
against opaque architecture and visible through glass. Lower cameras extend
to2800m to contain the1900m renderer; the pond retains its4000m range. Loading
the catalogue never blocks city startup; failure reports an honest status.
No voices, accounts, location permissions or private storage are used.

## Immersive telescope

The study reader offers an optional full-screen sky view. It preserves the real player position, uses the same measured catalogue, and offers a 12–100 degree field of view, removable guide lines, study and photo actions. Arrows/left stick pan the optical view; drag/right stick retain normal look control. Returning restores the physical observatory and normal camera. Native browser fullscreen is requested only by that explicit button and falls back to a viewport-filling view if unavailable. The code retains user-owned fullscreen and compensates for a request resolving after exit/disposal. The immersive scene hides architecture only during its own render and restores previous visibility before the next scene update and on exit.

The structural deck thickness is shared in NIGHT_OBSERVATORY.deckThickness.
Its underside now carries the bird ceiling mosaic atY21.875 and radius7.16.
The mosaic faces visitors below the deck; the telescope floor remains stone.
The flock approaches from beneath rather than crossing through the platform.
