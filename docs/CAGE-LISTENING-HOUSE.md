# Cage: the frame is part of the work

The House of Listening teaches a distinction: chance can choose a sequence,
while a composer still chooses its rules and a listener chooses what to notice.
It is an original learning encounter, not a reconstructed Cage home, historical
score or recording.

## What visitors do and see

An upright two-row comparison board makes the room's purpose visible from the
walking aisle. The supported physical score table uses pitch-specific colours,
flat rest blocks and a playback cursor. The same sixteen seeded events can stay
in their generated order, have their rests spread, or have all rests gathered.
The pitch inventory, rest count, relative order of sounding events and four-second
duration stay identical. The visitor predicts which arrangement has a longer
uninterrupted rest, checks the exact finite answer, and may compare the quiet
optional synthesized versions. This is a comparison of temporal framing, not a
test of musical taste or a statistical proof about the generator.

Stop generated sound opens an untimed prompt to notice near, distant and initially
ignored sounds. The physical notes disappear and the board changes to a listening
frame. The visitor chooses the interval; nothing waits for a timer, records the
room, requests microphone access or awards a completion. Hearing is optional:
the visual event inventory and arrangements remain comparable without audio.
The existing private notebook saves only an explicitly chosen reflection.

## Historical grounding and limits

MoMA describes 4′33″ as a framing of environmental sounds and identifies the
surviving proportional-notation version. The John Cage Trust catalogue relates
the work to Cage's use of chance in sounds and silences. These support the
house's attention/chance/framing distinction; the city's exercise itself is new.

- MoMA, *4′33″ (In Proportional Notation)*, 1952/1953:
  https://www.moma.org/collection/works/163616
- John Cage Trust, *4′33″*, complete-works record:
  https://data-johncage.org/pp/John-Cage-Work-Detail.cfm?work_ID=17

No historical score image, performance or long quotation is reproduced. The
sixteen-slot deterministic generator is not an I Ching implementation, physical
randomness, or a simulation of Cage's complete practice. No existing Kokoro voice,
recording, private account connection or notebook storage scheme is changed.

## Verification

`tests/cage-listening.mjs` exhausts all 9999 allowed seeds to check preservation of
each event multiset, sounding-event order and duration. It checks exact rest-run
comparisons, guards and four-language copy parity. The actual seminar UI test
exercises arrangements, prediction feedback, the physical comparison board,
listening/return, optional sound, existing notes, playback and cleanup. The
existing source scene suite checks doors, aisle, geometry and draw budget.
Browser inspection and publication remain the coordinating agent's responsibility.
