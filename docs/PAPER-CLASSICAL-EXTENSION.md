# Classical paper gallery extension

Eight additional original historical works, prepared on 17 November 2026, give
the Foundations and Geometry floors a connected reading route. This is a
curation, not an objective ranking of the best papers. The short English
questions in `historic-papers-classical-data.js` are original writing for the
city. Historical pages retain their original German or Latin printing; no
modern translation, reconstructed cover, or archive metadata cover is displayed.

All eight images are local WebP assets, height 1600 pixels, quality 90. Their
original margins, page furniture, signatures and footers are retained. The only
operations are page extraction, resizing and format conversion. First-page
means the opening historical text leaf specified below, not necessarily PDF
page 1. The pamphlets/monographs are described honestly as such.

The sequence supports comparison: notation and inference, arithmetic axioms,
continuity and analytic proof, an open research programme; then geometric
definitions, combinatorial invariants, intrinsic curvature and transformation
groups. It intentionally extends the existing Boole, Dedekind, Riemann, Fourier
and Noether displays rather than replacing them.

## Sources and reuse evidence

These are public-domain original works and faithful mechanical reproductions.
No right to republish is inferred merely from a free download button.

- **Frege:** [SUB Göttingen original record](https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN538957069),
  original-scan PDF distributed by [Zenodo record 6330677](https://zenodo.org/records/6330677).
  [Commons’ original 1879 edition record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Begriffsschrift.djvu)
  explicitly identifies Public Domain Mark 1.0. Frege died in 1925; the 1879
  text is public domain in the source country and the US. Only the original
  historical scan leaf is extracted from the larger PDF; its modern metadata
  and editorial cover pages are excluded. A different PDF circulating under
  the name `Frege_Begriffsschrift.pdf` was inspected and rejected because it is
  a modern English translation. It is not a source for the published asset.
- **Peano:** [original Harvard/Google scan at Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/arithmeticespri00peangoog).
  [Commons reuse record from this same original work](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Arithmetices_principia_Properties_of_equality.png)
  identifies the 1889 original and its US public-domain status. Peano died in
  1932, so the original text is also out of copyright under Italy’s life-plus-70
  term. The page is a faithful scan, not a modern transcription, translation,
  or creative edition. Harvard University copy; digitized by Google.
- **Hilbert:** [GDZ volume and exact leaf](https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN252457811_1900?tify=%7B%22pages%22%3A%5B269%5D%7D),
  [Commons original article and PDM declaration](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hilbert_-_Mathematische_Probleme.pdf).
  Hilbert died in 1943; original article published in 1900. The asset was
  obtained as the matching GDZ original JPEG, not from the site's HTML text.
- **Bolzano:** [ETH record / DOI](https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/doi/10.3931/e-rara-100813),
  ETH-Bibliothek Zürich **Rar 10776**. Its [METS record](https://www.e-rara.ch/oai?verb=GetRecord&metadataPrefix=mets&identifier=27886662)
  explicitly has `accessCondition type="use and reproduction"` pointing to
  `https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/` and the value `PDM 1.0`;
  the DFG rights section also has `dv:license` = `pdm`.
- **Gauss:** [ETH record / DOI and explicit PDM declaration](https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/doi/10.3931/e-rara-59755),
  ETH-Bibliothek Zürich **Rar 21533**. [Original IIIF manifest](https://www.e-rara.ch/i3f/v20/16144729/manifest).
- **Klein:** [Commons original-scan record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vergleichende_betrachtungen_%C3%BCber_neuere_geometrische_forsuchungen_.._(IA_abn7632.0001.001.umich.edu).pdf)
  marks the 1872 edition public domain in the US. Klein died in 1925, so it is
  also out of copyright in Germany under the life-plus-70 term. Original
  University of Michigan copy, digitized by Internet Archive. The original
  German scan is used, with no later translation.
- **Euclid:** [ETH record / DOI and explicit PDM declaration](https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/content/titleinfo/2553854),
  ETH-Bibliothek Zürich **Rar 1919**, DOI **10.3931/e-rara-8935**.
  [Original IIIF manifest](https://www.e-rara.ch/i3f/v20/2553854/manifest).
  Credits include Adelard of Bath, Campanus of Novara and printer Erhard Ratdolt.
- **Euler:** [Euler Archive bibliographic authority](https://scholarlycommons.pacific.edu/euler-works/230/)
  establishes original title, 1758 publication, Latin language and pages
  109–140. [Original journal volume scan](https://archive.org/details/novicommentar417521753imp).
  [Commons reuse record for a plate from this same E230 article](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Euler_Polyeder.pdf)
  identifies public-domain status, including Euler’s death in 1783. The
  displayed leaf comes from the original eighteenth-century printing, not the
  later *Opera Omnia* edition or the modern German translation.

ETH asks, as customary scholarly practice, for the author, holding institution
and persistent identifier to accompany reuse. Those credits are retained in
every relevant display record and this document. Public Domain Mark is a status
label, not a new exclusive copyright license.

## Acquisition and reproducibility

Development sources and contact sheets are staged in
`work/paper-extension-sources/` and are not shipped as large PDFs. The local
preparation script `work/render-classical-pages.py` uses Poppler page extraction
and Pillow resizing. The contact sheet `classical-contact-sheet.jpg` was
visually inspected against all eight identities. Inspection caught an initial
Euler page-offset error (p. 110 rather than p. 109); the final asset was rerendered
from PDF p. 189 and its heading, original p. 109 and footer were inspected again.
