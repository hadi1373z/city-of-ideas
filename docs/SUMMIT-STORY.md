# Summit story and city design update

Choose **Summit story** on the welcome screen, then **Begin the story ascent**. Climb the four-turn mountain manually or use the existing route tour. Reaching the summit triggers one transformation; reading every chapter is optional. **Free exploration** retains the original visible figure and the optional roof invitation.

The child pauses, crawls along the straight roof route, waits beside the gallery opening, and leaps into the crater. A single smooth golden flare marks its disappearance; only then does the Übermensch rise. The figure is clipped below the molten surface during its emergence. The child’s leap clears the courtyard railing, and hidden figures do not leave an invisible walking obstacle.

The visitor keeps control of their position. **Follow the scene** offers a separate camera; walking or dragging restores the visitor’s view. The final view can be held until **Return to my view** is selected. The chapter panel and ordinary route buttons recede during the sequence. **Skip animation** reaches the same final state. **Replay transformation** resets both figures, and is disabled if the visitor stands on the child’s starting place. Leaving an active event resolves it to a complete tableau. Reduced-motion mode omits the leap, flare, and camera travel.

Opening a dialog, reading panel, or exhibit pauses the event. The scene runs once per visit, without resetting on another summit crossing. This is an original artistic scene inspired by Zarathustra, not an illustration claimed to reproduce a passage. English, Persian, and German controls are included.

The shared roof and bridge keep their centered geometry and matching circles. The mountain trail uses the same quiet tile pattern in a darker stone tone. Retaining faces have mineral color variation, the child has a softer bronze finish, and the entrance landscape groups trees with varied crowns while leaving the doors and walking paths clear. Lighting is softer and the sky is lighter.

Validation includes the ordered event, pause/yield/skip/replay paths, reduced motion, courtyard railing clearance, independent camera and visitor pose, and the existing roof, animal-corridor, bridge symmetry, and landscape checks. Browser checks separately cover the complete sequence and responsive controls.
