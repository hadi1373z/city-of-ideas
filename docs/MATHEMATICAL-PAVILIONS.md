# Hexagonal mathematical pavilions

Three original hexagonal pavilions form a northern crescent around Zarathustra Gallery, avoiding the artist and study houses. Open doorways face the gallery. Aperiodic Order, Inflation and Local Symmetry have vaulted, pointed and saddle ceilings made from the same finite Robinson-triangle substitution patches as their floors. Each offers levels 1–4 and floor/ceiling viewing controls. Geometry uses a golden-ratio subdivision, then crops it to the hexagon. Rhombs at the boundary are partial; counts explicitly describe triangles before cropping. The text does not claim that bare rhomb shapes enforce aperiodicity, or that a finite patch proves an infinite property.

Reference: https://tilings.math.uni-bielefeld.de/glossary/matching-rules/ . All geometry, prompts and explanatory text are original; no external pictures are republished. No voices, private saves or accounts are changed.
