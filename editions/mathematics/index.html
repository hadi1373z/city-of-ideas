A city of connected ideas

Travel to a real doorstep, or follow the streets there on foot. Six shared gardens connect neighbouring houses to the outer promenade. Each experiment leads to a question; a visit alone earns no completion.

My mathematical arguments

Your campus notes are private to this browser. Only “Keep my argument” saves your writing. Proof Atelier arguments keep their established save and resume settings.

Take your time

Walk with W A S D or the arrow keys. Drag the view to look around. On a phone, hold the walking arrows and drag elsewhere to look. E opens a nearby house or workbench. Escape closes a panel.

The map offers direct travel to real doorsteps. Every building has a level entrance. Experiments pause walking while you read. No notifications cover the walking arrows.

Begin with any house. For a longer investigation, enter the central Proof Atelier: six workshops connect invariants, Hall’s theorem, exchanges, covers, prices and a final graph certificate.
